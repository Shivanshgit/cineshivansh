Fairing touring windscreen

Dochaki, now DMotoTech · 15-inch

The fairing shape cuts the air from the middle, so there's far less wind buffeting on your chest. At 5'7" the 15-inch fits me well, and install was pretty easy. Taller? Get the 17-inch, it suits riders around 5'11".

Small handlebar mirror

Wide angle blind spot mirror · rectangle convex, 360° adjustable

Stand on the pegs and the main mirror's line of sight is gone. This one still shows me what's behind. Base zip-tied to the bar, mirror sticks on.

About me

I'm Shivansh. I film my rides on the Himalayan 450: passes, roads, weather, and the people and food along the way.

I make these for riders in India planning their first or next long tour, and for people who love travel films but don't ride yet. Mostly helmet POV and wide shots. The footage does the talking.
